App Support

Need help with Family Path Locator? If you have questions about using the app, family groups, location sharing, account settings, notifications, safety alerts, or other app features, our support team is here to help.

  • Include your app version and device model when reporting a problem.
  • Describe the steps that led to the issue.
  • Never share your password or Family Code in a support request.

Permanent

Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your Family Path Locator account and associated data directly from the app when you are eligible. If you cannot complete the deletion from the app, you can contact us by email to request account deletion.
