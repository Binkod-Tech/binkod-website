Your Account

You are responsible for your account. That means:

  • Keeping your sign-in details private and secure.
  • Making sure the information you provide is accurate.
  • Everything that happens while you are signed in through your account.
  • Telling us through Contact Us if you think your account is being used without your permission.
  • The Family Code is used to join a family — it is not a login method and does not give anyone access to your account.

Family Groups

Family Path Locator lets you create or join a family group:

  • Only join families you are meant to be part of, and share a Family Code only with the people you want in your family.
  • Administrators can manage roles, remove members and delete a family once it is empty; members can leave a family at any time.
  • The current free plan allows a limited number of families per account and members per family.
  • Do not try to reach, view or use another person's information or account without their permission.

Location Sharing

Location features depend on your device permissions, your device settings and the features you switch on. Please use location sharing responsibly and respectfully:

  • Do not use Family Path Locator to harass, threaten or track anyone against their wishes.
  • Location information may not always be accurate or immediately available. GPS accuracy, network conditions, device settings, battery, connectivity and the environment around you can all affect what is shown.

Safety Alerts

Safety Alerts let you send a message and your last known location to your family so they know you need help. This is a family communication and notification feature only — Family Path Locator is not an emergency service and does not replace one. If you need immediate help, contact your local emergency services.
