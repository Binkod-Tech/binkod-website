Features

Everything a family needs to stay connected

A clear look at what Family Path Locator can do for your family — what each feature does and what it depends on.

Family Location Sharing

See your family on a shared map. Every member who has location sharing switched on appears with their current location, so you can check where everyone is and when their location last updated.

Each member stays in control of whether they share. Sharing needs location permission and the right device settings, so what you see depends on the other person's device and connection.

Family Code

Create a family and receive an eight-character Family Code. Share the code with the people you trust — anyone with the code can join your family by entering it, and you stay connected with the members of your family.

Eight-character code Join with a code

Safety Alerts

Send a Safety Alert to your family when you need attention or assistance. Choose an alert type, add an optional message, and include your current location so your family knows where you are.

Safety Alerts require internet connectivity and notification permissions. They are not a replacement for emergency services.

Family Messages

Send a message to an individual member of your family or to your whole family group. Only members of your family are available for messaging, and messages are exchanged within the application.

While the application is open you may hear incoming message sounds, depending on your device's sound settings. Ordinary messages do not send push notifications when you are not using the application.

GeoTag Camera

Take photos with location-related details attached, so every photo is tied to the place where it was captured. The details are stamped onto the picture itself: your name, family, address, coordinates, and the date and time.

  • Works when location permission and the relevant device settings are enabled.
  • Photos are stored on your device — the application does not upload them to a server or cloud.
  • Manage and delete your stored photos whenever you like.
  • Share with others using your device's usual share options — copies you share are then handled by the apps you share them with.
Example stamp Your name · Your family · Current address 0.0000, 0.0000 Date · Time

Background Location Sharing

Allow background location access so your current location can keep sharing while the application is not on screen.

Depends on your permissions, your device's settings and its operating-system restrictions, so it may not continue on every device. Sharing stays under your control and can be switched off at any time.

Weather

Current weather for you and your family — temperature, feels-like, condition, humidity, cloud cover, wind, and rain.

Current conditions only. Requires internet, uses third-party weather data, and weather sharing can be turned off.

Member Status

Useful status at a glance — location freshness, distance, battery level, charging state, location accuracy, and weather.

Battery and weather sharing are controlled by consent settings.

Offline AI Assistant

A built-in assistant that helps you with supported questions about the application, without needing an internet connection for that offline functionality.

It runs on your device for its supported functionality — not every possible question is available offline.

Privacy & User Control

Manage your preferences in Settings — location sharing, battery-status sharing, weather updates, and more. Leave a family whenever you like, and delete your account at any time.

Only current location is stored — Family Path Locator does not save a location history or timeline.

Account Management

Sign in with Google or email and password, with email verification and password reset supported.

Account deletion is available in the application.

How it works

Up and running in five simple steps

From installing the application to exploring its features — the whole Family Path Locator journey.

  1. 1

    Install Family Path Locator

    Get started by installing the application from Google Play on your device.

  2. 2

    Sign in or create an account

    Sign in with Google, or with your email address and password. New to the application? Create an account first, then sign in.

  3. 3

    Create or join a family

    Create a new family and the application generates an eight-character Family Code to share — or join an existing family by entering the Family Code someone you trust sent you.

  4. 4

    Connect with your family members

    Share your Family Code with the people you trust so they can join. Once your family is connected, you can stay in touch with the members of your family through the application's features.

  5. 5

    Explore the features

    Try live location sharing with family members who have sharing enabled, Family Messages for individual and family-group messages, GeoTag Camera photos stored on your device, Safety Alerts for your family, the Offline AI Assistant for supported questions, and Settings that keep your preferences under your control.

Privacy

Sharing that stays in the family

Family Path is designed around current location sharing within your family — with clear controls for what you share.

Family Path Locator does not save or maintain historical location records or a location timeline. It stores current location information for the live location-sharing feature — not a record of where someone has been.

  • Current location only
  • No location history
  • Location visible within your family group
  • Background location is user-controlled
  • Battery sharing has a consent toggle
  • Weather sharing has a consent toggle
  • GeoTag photos are not uploaded
  • Account deletion is supported

Good to know

Important details, up front

A few honest notes about how Family Path Locator works.

Location updates

Family Path Locator shows current shared locations rather than second-by-second tracking. Locations update as your device provides them while location sharing is on.

Safety Alerts

Safety Alerts require internet connectivity and appropriate notification permissions. They are not a replacement for emergency services.

Weather

Weather information is provided for informational purposes and depends on third-party weather data.

Photos

GeoTag photos remain on the user's device unless the user chooses to share them.

Android

The current application is available for Android.

Messages

Messages arrive within the application rather than as push notifications. Incoming message sounds may play while the application is open, depending on your device's sound settings.
