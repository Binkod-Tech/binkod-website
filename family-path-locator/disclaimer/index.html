Location

Information shown may therefore be delayed, approximate or unavailable at times, and should not be treated as guaranteed exact or continuous.

Safety Alerts

Safety Alerts are a family communication and notification feature. Family Path Locator is not an emergency response service and does not replace it. If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.
