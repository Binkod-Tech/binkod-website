Questions

Remove Ads FAQ.

How does Remove Ads work?

Remove Ads is a one-time, non-consumable purchase handled by Google Play. Nothing renews and there is no recurring charge.

Does Remove Ads unlock extra features?

No. Every information screen, guided check, report, analyzer and widget layout is available without a purchase. Remove Ads only turns advertising off.

Does the website show the price?

No. Google Play shows the localized price for your account and region before you buy. If Google Play returns no price, the app shows “Price unavailable” and disables buying.

Can I restore the purchase on a new phone?

Yes, with the same Google account. Install Device Matrix, sign in to Google Play with that account and use the restore purchase action in the app.

What is the refund policy?

Remove Ads purchases are processed by Google Play, and refunds are handled under Google Play's applicable refund policy. BINKOD TECH does not process payments or issue refunds directly. The Refunds section of the Privacy Policy has the full paragraph.

Does Remove Ads change the readings?

No. It does not change device readings, guided checks, reports, analyzers or widgets. It only stops ad requests.
