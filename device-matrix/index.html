The honest difference

Readings first. Guesses never.

A system-info app is only useful if you can trust what it shows. These are the rules Device Matrix follows on every screen.

Source

Read from Android, not invented

Device Matrix uses public Android interfaces available to normal apps. When the OS or manufacturer blocks something, the screen says so.

Status

“Not available on this device” is a real answer

A missing field is not quietly filled with a plausible-looking number. Some features and readings vary by brand, model and Android version.

Estimate

Estimates carry a label

Values such as device age and battery health can be approximate. The app marks estimates with ≈ and explains what they mean.

Local

Built to stay on the phone

There is no app account, backend or analytics service. Reports leave only when you copy, share or save them.

Open a clear detail screen for the parts Android will talk about: the device and build, processor and graphics, memory, storage, battery, heat, display, camera and sensors.

Network

See the connection without handing over the details.

Network shows the active link, metered and VPN state, operator information, DNS and Wi-Fi link details. Wi-Fi names, BSSID, IP addresses, gateway and DNS stay masked by default.

  • Masked network identifiers, revealable only when you choose.
  • Data usage for Today, 7 days and 30 days, split between Wi-Fi and mobile.
  • Connectivity information for Bluetooth and NFC where Android allows it.
  • Pro adds the Wi-Fi analyzer, with no scan and no permission request before Pro is verified.

Apps

Context for the apps that share your phone.

The Apps area lists applications with a launcher icon, then helps you see target API spread, installer mix, declared dangerous permissions and foreground time. Usage Access is a separate Android grant; the app explains it before sending you to Settings.

  • Search, sort and inspect launcher apps.
  • Permission analyzer groups apps by what their manifests declare.
  • Screen time shows foreground time, not screen-on time, and does not read app content.
  • No APK extraction and no app-termination tools.

Guided tests

A guided check-up for the screen, touch, sound, sensors, ports and radios.

Run one check or the full set. Each result can be passed, failed or left untested, and the app explains when Android cannot run a check on this device. Results stay on the phone and can be shared as a used-phone checklist.

  • Display and pixels, multi-touch, flashlight, loudspeaker, earpiece.
  • Proximity, light, accelerometer, charging, headset and USB audio.
  • Vibration, Bluetooth, volume buttons and microphone.
  • The microphone test moves a live meter only; audio is never recorded or sent.

    Questions

    Straight answers before you install.

    The questions people ask most often, answered on this page.

    What is Device Matrix?

    Device Matrix: System Info is an informational Android utility. It reads hardware, software, network and app details that Android exposes, guides optional hardware checks, builds local reports and offers home-screen widgets.

    Is Device Matrix a diagnostic or repair tool?

    No. It is an informational utility with guided checks. It does not diagnose a hardware fault, certify that a device is healthy or estimate repair cost.

    Which Android versions are supported?

    Android 8.0 and later. Some readings and permissions depend on the Android version and the manufacturer.

    Does it need root or a special build?

    No. Device Matrix uses public Android interfaces available to normal apps.

    Why does a reading say “Not available on this device”?

    Android or the manufacturer may not expose that field, the permission may not be granted, or the phone may not have the hardware. The app shows the limitation instead of guessing.

    Where do the readings come from?

    From public Android interfaces and the phone’s own sensors and system services. Values that are approximate are labelled.

    Does Device Matrix upload my readings?

    No. Readings are processed on the phone. The app has no Device Matrix account, backend for readings or analytics service. Reports leave only when you copy, share or save them.

    Why does it ask for location?

    Android treats Wi-Fi names and nearby-network scans as location data. The permission is requested only when you open Wi-Fi details or, with Pro, the Wi-Fi analyzer. Device Matrix does not use GPS coordinates as a reading.

    Does the microphone test record audio?

    No. It reads live sound levels to move a meter. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent, and the recorder is released when you leave the test or background the app.

    Does Device Matrix read my messages, photos or contacts?

    No. It does not read app content, keystrokes, messages, photos, contacts, SMS or call logs. It also does not request your IMEI, hardware serial number, Android ID, phone number or precise GPS coordinates. See the Privacy Policy for the full explanation.

    What are the guided hardware checks?

    Optional checks cover the display and pixels, multi-touch, flashlight, speaker, earpiece, proximity, light, accelerometer, charging, headset and USB audio, vibration, Bluetooth, volume buttons and microphone. Some checks depend on the hardware and Android version.

    Are test results sent anywhere?

    No. Results stay on the phone and can be shared as a checklist only when you choose.

    What is the health summary?

    A local summary calculated from the checks this phone can report. It is not a stored history, a diagnosis or a guarantee of device condition.

    What can I do with a report?

    Choose sections, preview the content, then copy or share the text or create a PDF. With Pro, you can export a custom PDF and a sanitised JSON file. The standard PDF always hides identifying details.

    Does the app show ads?

    Free is supported by Google AdMob ads, with Google’s consent form where required. Remove ads and Pro stop ad requests. Ads are not shown on Settings, permission explanations, hardware tests or live graphs.

    What do the plans include?

    Free includes the standard information screens, guided tests, reports and the smaller widgets. Remove ads stops advertising and removes the video gate from the standard PDF. Pro adds the Wi-Fi analyzer, larger widgets, more appearance options and custom exports.

    Is Pro a subscription?

    No. Remove ads and Pro are separate one-time purchases through Google Play. Nothing renews. A purchase is linked to the Google account used in Google Play and can be restored with that account.

    How do widgets work?

    Free includes 2×1 and 4×1 RAM, storage and battery widgets. Pro adds 2×2 standard widgets and the 4×2 Device Health Check. Add widgets from your launcher’s widget picker; Device Matrix requests updates, but the launcher controls refresh timing.

    Does Device Matrix change my phone or promise how it will run?

    No. It does not silently change settings, clear memory, repair hardware or promise that a reading will make the phone faster or last longer. Where Android needs a decision, the app opens the relevant screen, and you stay in control.

    How do I contact support?

    Email binkodtech@gmail.com with your app version, device model and the steps that led to the problem. Do not send Google account passwords or payment details.
