Privacy Policy

Introduction

Device Matrix reads information about your phone on the phone itself. There is no Device Matrix account, no app backend, no cloud storage for your readings and no analytics service in the app. Your readings are not sent to us.

Google services are part of the app experience. Google AdMob serves ads, and Google Play handles the optional one-time Remove ads and Pro purchases. Google Play processes the payment; the app never receives your card number. Google may use data for advertising as described below, and where the law requires a choice, Google’s consent form asks before an ad is requested.

The app does not sell your personal data. The app’s own reading code does not request your IMEI, hardware serial number, Android ID, phone number, contacts, SMS, camera images, precise GPS coordinates or the phone’s own Wi-Fi/Bluetooth MAC address.

What the app reads

Device Matrix reads what Android reports about the device and its software: model and build, Android version, processor and graphics, memory, storage, battery and thermal state, display, camera and sensor capabilities, network and Wi-Fi details, launcher-app information and sizes where Usage Access is granted, and the results of hardware checks you run.

Readings use public Android interfaces available to normal apps. When Android or the manufacturer does not expose a value, the app shows “Not available on this device” instead of guessing or filling the gap with a hidden estimate.

Some values are clearly labelled estimates, for example device age and the battery-health percentage. The Home health score is calculated on the phone from the checks that could be read. It is a summary of those readings, not a diagnosis or a safety assessment.

What stays on your phone

Theme, accent, unit choices, dashboard layout, saved hardware-test results, report previews and widget appearance are stored in the app’s private storage on this phone. Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app removes them.

If you buy Remove ads or Pro, a short-lived purchase proof for each product is also kept on this phone: the product id, a SHA-256 hash of the purchase token (never the token itself), verification timestamps and an installation id. It is authenticated with a key held by Android Keystore, expires after at most 30 days and is never included in Android backup. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes it. The purchase itself remains in your Google Play account.

A report or checklist is built on this phone. Text leaves only when you tap Copy or Share, and a PDF leaves only through Android’s share sheet. You choose the destination.

We do not operate a server that receives your readings. There is no crash-reporting or analytics SDK in this build.

Sensitive values and exports

Values that can identify a network or a build are always masked in the app. These include Wi-Fi names (SSID), BSSID values, IP addresses, the gateway, DNS servers, the Android build fingerprint and nearby Wi-Fi names. Masking cannot be switched off in the app; a masked value can be revealed for a short time by tapping it, and copying is only possible while it is revealed.

Exported text and the preview follow a single “Include identifying details” switch, which is off by default. The standard PDF always replaces identifying details, even when that switch is on. Pro’s custom PDF and JSON follow the switch so the export matches the preview you approved.

The microphone test reads live sound levels only to move a meter. Audio is never recorded to a file, stored or sent anywhere. The recorder is released when you leave the test or the app goes to the background.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Internet, network state and Wi-Fi state are used for advertising, Google Play purchase checks and network readings. The ads SDK may use the advertising ID through the Google-provided AD_ID permission.
  • Google Play Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING) is a normal install-time permission. It is used only to load the Remove ads and Pro prices, start the Google Play purchase sheet, restore a purchase and check ownership. It does not give the app access to your payment details.
  • Vibration is used for haptics and the vibration test. Set alarm is used only when you create an alarm or timer. NFC is read-only and used only when you open the NFC information. Modify audio settings is used only by the earpiece test.
  • Location is requested only when you open Wi-Fi details or, with Pro, the Wi-Fi analyzer, because Android treats Wi-Fi names and nearby-network scans as location data. The app does not use GPS coordinates as a reading. The Pro analyzer does not scan or ask for permission until Pro ownership is verified. Nearby Wi-Fi Devices is used only for the Android 13+ nearby-network list.
  • Bluetooth Connect is requested only to read whether Bluetooth is on and whether LE is supported. The app does not scan for or connect to Bluetooth devices.
  • Microphone is requested only after you start the microphone test and confirm the explanation. You can deny it; the test then shows “Not tested” and a shortcut to system Settings.
  • Usage Access is a special Android access that you choose to grant in Settings. It is used only for the Apps tab’s sizes, screen time and permission analysis.

The app does not ask for all-package access, phone state, Camera, contacts, SMS, overlay permission or access to the phone’s own hardware identifiers.

Advertising and your choices

The app is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may collect or receive your device’s advertising ID, basic device and app information, approximate location and diagnostics, as described in Google’s own privacy policy.

Where required by law, for example in the EEA and the UK, the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent form (Google UMP) before requesting an ad. You can change or withdraw that choice later. When Google reports that a privacy-options form is required, a Manage ad choices button appears at the end of the in-app policy and opens Google’s form.

A free user may choose to watch a rewarded video to create a standard PDF. The video is optional; if it cannot load, the PDF opens after a short fallback. Remove ads and Pro skip the video.

If you buy Remove ads or Pro, the app stops requesting ads and hides the ad-choices button because there are no ads to configure. Android Settings still controls your advertising ID for other apps.

You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Remove ads and Pro purchases

Remove ads and Pro are separate one-time, lifetime, non-consumable purchases handled by Google Play. Remove ads turns ads off. Pro turns ads off and unlocks the 2×2 and 4×2 widgets, extra accent colours, dashboard layout editing, the Animations Off setting, the Wi-Fi analyzer and custom PDF/JSON report exports. There is no subscription and nothing renews.

The price shown for each plan is the localized price Google Play returns for your account and region. If Google Play returns no price, the plan says “Price unavailable” and cannot be bought. Google processes the payment; Device Matrix never receives or stores your card number or full billing address.

To decide which features are available, the app asks Google Play whether the Google account signed in on this phone owns each product. If you own Remove ads and later buy the Pro upgrade, that combination counts as Pro. Apart from the protected local proof described above, the app sends no purchase information to us and has no purchase backend of its own.

Reinstalling the app or using another phone signed in to the same Google account can restore these purchases through Google Play. A different Google account does not inherit them. Refunds and payment disputes are handled by Google Play; if a purchase is refunded, ads can return and Pro features can lock again after the next ownership check.

If Google Play cannot be reached, a verified local proof for each product can keep a plan active for up to 30 days. After that, the app waits for Google Play to confirm the purchase again before keeping ads off or unlocking Pro features. A purchase is never granted by the JavaScript interface alone.

No Refund Policy

Remove ads and Pro are digital upgrades sold through Google Play. Binkod Tech does not process the payment and cannot issue a refund directly.

All purchases are subject to Google Play’s refund policy and any rights provided by applicable consumer law. To request a refund or dispute a charge, use Google Play purchase history or Google Play support. We do not offer a separate refund, credit or exchange outside Google Play.

If Google Play approves a refund, ads can return and Pro features can lock again after the next ownership check. For help finding the right Google Play support option, contact us using the details below.

Home-screen widgets

Device Matrix includes home-screen widgets for RAM, storage, battery and a health summary. They read the same local values as the app and show them in your launcher. Nothing from a widget is uploaded.

The 2×2 and 4×2 widget sizes are Pro features. On a Free or Remove ads install, Android draws those widgets with a locked message; the smaller 2×1 and 4×1 sizes stay free. The native widget renderer checks the protected local proof, not a setting that JavaScript can simply switch on.

The storage unit and widget appearance choices are kept in the app’s private storage so widgets format sizes and colours consistently. Android and your launcher decide when a widget refreshes; the app requests updates periodically. A widget may therefore show the values from its last update until the launcher refreshes it.

Widgets cannot be added from inside the app. You add them from your launcher’s widget picker.

Children

Device Matrix is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Keeping and deleting data

Because there is no backend, there is no server-side copy of your readings to delete. Local settings, saved test results, widget preferences and the Remove ads or Pro purchase proofs remain in the app’s private storage until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. Reports you copied or shared are controlled by the app or service you sent them to.

Deleting the app does not delete the purchase from your Google Play account. After reinstalling, sign in with the same account and use Restore purchase. Google Play keeps its own purchase records under Google’s terms and privacy policy.

Android protects app-private storage, but no software can promise perfect security. Keep your phone and its screen lock up to date.

Accuracy and third-party names

Readings vary by brand, model, Android version and the permissions granted. Some values are estimates and are labelled as such. “Not available on this device” means Android did not report the value. Device Matrix is an informational app, not a diagnostic, repair or safety tool.

Android, Google, AdMob and the names of device makers, chipset makers and GPU families are trademarks or registered trademarks of their respective owners. Device Matrix uses them only to identify the hardware and software that reported the reading, with no endorsement or affiliation implied.
