Introduction

Device Matrix: System Info is a local device-information application for Android. It reads information that Android exposes about the phone and presents it on the phone. There is no Device Matrix account, no application backend, no cloud storage for your readings, and no analytics or crash-reporting service in the app.

Google AdMob serves advertising, and Google Play handles the optional Remove Ads purchase and payment. Google may process data for advertising as described below. Where the law requires a choice, Google's consent form asks before an ad is requested.

What the app reads

Using public Android interfaces available to normal apps, Device Matrix can access the following device information:

  • Device and software: brand, model, manufacturer, Android version, security patch, build and kernel details, and an estimated device age.
  • Processor and graphics: chipset name where available, core count, supported ABIs, core clusters, current and maximum frequencies, GPU renderer and graphics API versions.
  • Memory and storage: total, used and available RAM, low-memory and swap details where reported, internal capacity, used and free space, and app or media breakdowns where Usage Access allows.
  • Battery and thermal: charge level, charging state, current, voltage, power, temperature, technology, capacity, cycle count, battery-saver state and thermal status where reported.
  • Display, camera and sensors: resolution, density, refresh rate, HDR support and display modes; camera and lens characteristics reported by Android; and a searchable sensor list with live values where supported.
  • Network and connectivity: active connection type, metered, VPN, Data Saver and airplane-mode state, operator and roaming state, Private DNS, IP and Wi-Fi link details, data-usage totals, and Bluetooth or NFC state where available.
  • Apps and checks: launcher-app information, sizes, screen time and permission analysis where Usage Access is granted, and the results of the hardware checks you choose to run.

When Android or the manufacturer does not expose a value, the app shows “Not available on this device” instead of guessing. Some values, such as device age and battery health, are estimates and are labelled as such. The Home health score is a local summary of the checks the phone can report; it is not a diagnosis or a safety assessment.

The app does not request your phone number, contacts, messages, call logs, photos, camera images or the phone's hardware identifiers. It does not read the content of other apps. Android location permission is used only for Wi-Fi names and nearby-network scans; the app does not use GPS coordinates as a reading.

What stays on your phone

Settings, theme, accent, unit choices, dashboard layout, saved hardware-test results, report previews and widget preferences are stored in the app's private storage on the phone. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes them.

If you buy Remove Ads, the app keeps a small local record of the purchase so it can keep ads off when Google Play is temporarily unavailable. The purchase itself remains in your Google Play account. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the local record.

A report or checklist is built on the phone. Text leaves only when you tap Copy, Download or Share, and a PDF or JSON file leaves only through Android's share sheet or a Downloads save. You choose the destination. Review the preview before exporting or sharing.

Device Matrix does not operate a server that receives your readings.

Your controls and exports

Values that can identify a network or a build are masked by default. Tap a masked value to reveal it briefly; copying is only possible while it is revealed.

A single “Include sensitive information” switch controls whether identifying values appear in the preview and exports. It is off by default. Review any report before sharing it.

The microphone test reads live sound levels only to move a meter. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • Internet, network state and Wi-Fi state: used for advertising, Google Play purchase checks and network readings.
  • Google Play Billing: used only for the optional Remove Ads purchase and restore. The app never receives your payment details.
  • Vibration, set alarm, NFC and modify audio settings: used for haptics, the vibration test, alarm or timer creation, read-only NFC information and the earpiece test.
  • Location: requested only when you open Wi-Fi details or the Wi-Fi analyzer, because Android treats Wi-Fi names and nearby-network scans as location data. Precise and approximate location are requested together. The app does not use GPS coordinates as a reading and never switches Wi-Fi on or off or connects to a network.
  • Bluetooth Connect: requested only to read whether Bluetooth is on and whether LE is supported. The app does not scan for or connect to Bluetooth devices.
  • Microphone: requested only after you start the microphone test and confirm the explanation. You can deny it; the test then shows “Not tested” and a shortcut to system Settings.
  • Usage Access: a special Android access that you choose to grant in Settings. It is used only for the Apps tab's sizes, screen time and permission analysis.

The app does not ask for all-package access, phone state, Camera, contacts, SMS, overlay permission or access to the phone's hardware identifiers.

Advertising and third-party services

The app is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect or receive device and app information, approximate location and diagnostics, as described in Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Where required by law, for example in the EEA and the UK, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before requesting an ad. You can change or withdraw that choice later. When Google reports that a privacy-options form is required, a Manage ad choices button appears in Settings and opens Google's form.

A free user may choose to watch a rewarded video for Generate PDF. The PDF is generated only after Google confirms the reward; if the video cannot load, is closed early or fails, nothing is exported and the app offers a retry. Remove Ads users skip the video.

On a free install, some report actions and the Wi-Fi, App and Permission analyzers and Screen Time may show an interstitial. Remove Ads skips every ad.

If you buy Remove Ads, the app stops requesting ads and hides the ad-choices button because there are no ads to configure. Android Settings still controls ad personalization for other apps.

Remove Ads purchase

Remove Ads is one optional, one-time, non-consumable purchase handled by Google Play. It turns advertising off. It does not unlock or restrict any application feature. Every information screen, guided check, report, analyzer and widget layout is available without it. Nothing renews.

Google Play processes the payment and shows the localized price for your account and region. Device Matrix never receives or stores your card number or full billing address. The purchase is linked to the Google account used in Google Play, and reinstalling the app or using another phone signed in to the same account can restore it. If the purchase is refunded, ads can return.

Device information and accuracy

Device Matrix is an informational utility. It reports values that Android and the device make available through public interfaces. Readings and features depend on the device manufacturer, the Android version, the permissions you grant and the APIs the platform exposes. Some readings may be unavailable, incomplete, rounded, delayed or approximate, and some features may not work on every device. “Not available on this device” means Android or the manufacturer did not expose a value.

The app does not guarantee hardware health, device performance, security or diagnostic accuracy. A reading, health score or test result is not a certification that a device is healthy, fast, secure or faulty, and it is not a repair estimate. Do not rely on the app for medical, safety, legal or other critical decisions.
