Privacy Policy

This policy describes the Android app Device Matrix: System Info, developed by Binkod Tech. It explains what the app reads, what stays on the phone, the Google services involved and the choices available to you.

Introduction

Device Matrix reads information about your phone on the phone itself. There is no account, no backend, no cloud storage service. Your readings are not sent to us.

Two Google services are involved: Google AdMob serves ads, and Google Play handles the optional one-time Remove Ads and Pro Version purchases. Google Play processes the payment; the app never receives your card number. Google may use data for advertising as described below, and you can manage ad choices where the law requires a choice.

The app itself does not sell your personal data. It does not read your IMEI, hardware serial number, phone number, contacts, SMS or precise location.

What the app reads

Device Matrix reads what Android reports about the device and its software: model and build, Android version, CPU and GPU, memory, storage, battery and thermal state, display, camera and sensor capabilities, network and Wi-Fi details, installed-app sizes where Usage Access is granted, and the results of hardware checks you run.

Readings use public Android interfaces available to normal apps. When Android or the manufacturer does not expose a value, the app shows “Not available on this device” instead of guessing or filling the gap with a silent estimate.

Some values are clearly labelled estimates, for example device age and the battery-health percentage. The Home health score is calculated on this phone from the checks that could be read.

What stays on your phone

Theme, accent, unit choices, dashboard layout, saved hardware-test results and widget appearance are stored in the app’s private storage on this phone. Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app removes them.

If you buy Remove Ads or Pro Version, a short-lived purchase proof for each product is also kept on this phone: the product id, a SHA-256 hash of the purchase token (never the token itself), verification timestamps and an installation id. It is authenticated with a key held by Android Keystore, expires after at most 30 days and is never included in Android backup. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes it.

A report or checklist is built on this phone. Copy puts the preview text on the clipboard. Share and Download actions run only when you tap them. Download saves a text, PDF or JSON file to Downloads/Device Matrix on Android 10 and later; Share opens Android’s share sheet. You choose whether to share a file. Optional report details (a brand or name and a note) stay in memory for the current report and are never saved as a setting or sent anywhere except inside the export you create.

We do not operate a server that receives your readings. There is no crash-reporting or analytics SDK in this build.

Sensitive values and exports

Values that can identify a network or a build are always masked in the app. These include Wi-Fi names (SSID), BSSID values, IP addresses, the gateway, DNS servers, the Android build fingerprint and nearby Wi-Fi names. Masking cannot be switched off; a masked value can be revealed for a short time by tapping it, and copying is only possible while it is revealed.

Exports use one Sensitive Information switch, off by default. When off, the Wi-Fi network name, IP address, subnet, gateway, DNS, Private DNS, proxy, mobile operator and build fingerprint values show as ******** in the preview, copied text, downloaded and shared text, standard and custom PDFs, JSON and export metadata. The labels remain visible so it is clear the value was deliberately withheld; non-sensitive values remain. The standard PDF follows the same switch as every other export.

PDF, text and JSON filenames start with the device name shown on Home, followed by “Device Matrix”, a local timestamp and random letters. They never contain a network name, optional report detail, account or other personal information. Report metadata likewise contains no network or account identity.

The microphone test reads live sound levels only to move a meter. Audio is never recorded to a file, stored or sent anywhere. The recorder is released when you leave the test or the app goes to the background.

The biometrics check only asks Android whether biometric hardware and an enrolled biometric are present. It never starts an authentication prompt, reads a fingerprint or face image, receives a biometric template, or stores biometric data.

Permissions and why they are needed

The app requests only the permissions needed by features that exist in this build. Runtime permissions are requested when you open the matching feature, never at startup. Install-time permissions cannot be denied at runtime; if Android or Google Play services do not provide the related capability, the feature shows “Not available on this device”.

The app does not ask for contacts, SMS, phone state, camera, overlay, access to all installed packages, or access to shared storage. It does not run a foreground service or wake lock.

Permission by permission

INTERNET. Access: opens network connections. Feature: Google AdMob ads and Google Play Billing price, purchase, restore and verification checks. Data: ad and purchase requests go to Google; your device readings are not sent to us. Optionality: install-time permission, so there is no runtime prompt; Remove Ads or Pro Version stops ad requests. If unavailable: prices may show “Price unavailable” and ads may not load, while local readings still work.

ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE. Access: the active connection type and whether a network is available. Feature: connectivity readings and avoiding ad requests while offline. Data: local only. Optionality: install-time permission with no runtime prompt. If unavailable: the connection type shows “Not available on this device” and ads may fail to load.

ACCESS_WIFI_STATE. Access: Wi-Fi state and Android’s scan cache. Feature: Wi-Fi details and the Pro Version Wi-Fi Analyzer. Data: local only; Android returns the list to this app and nothing is uploaded. Optionality: install-time permission with no runtime prompt. If unavailable: Wi-Fi sections show “Not available on this device”.

AD_ID. Access: the Android advertising ID through Google’s ads permission. Feature: ads only. Data: sent to Google with ad requests; no other feature reads it. Optionality: you can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android Settings; Remove Ads or Pro Version stops ad requests. If unavailable: ads may be less relevant or may not load, and the rest of the app works.

SET_ALARM. Access: opens your clock app with a prefilled alarm or timer. Feature: Set alarm and Timer. Data: a local hand-off; Device Matrix stores neither the alarm nor the timer. Optionality: install-time permission; you can still cancel in the clock app. If unavailable: the app says that no clock app could be opened.

VIBRATE. Access: the vibration motor. Feature: press haptics and the vibration test. Data: local only. Optionality: install-time permission; Android’s touch-vibration setting can silence haptics. If unavailable: the test reports that vibration is not available.

NFC. Access: read-only NFC state and capabilities. Feature: NFC information. Data: local only. Optionality: install-time permission; it only works on devices with NFC hardware. If unavailable: NFC information shows “Not available on this device”.

MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS. Access: switches audio routing for the earpiece check. Feature: earpiece test only. Data: local only. Optionality: install-time permission with no runtime prompt. If unavailable: the earpiece test shows “Not available on this device”.

com.android.vending.BILLING. Access: Google Play Billing. Feature: loading prices, buying, restoring and verifying Remove Ads and Pro Version. Data: Google Play processes the payment; the app keeps a protected local proof and sends no purchase information to us. Optionality: a normal install-time permission and buying is optional. If unavailable, or if no Google account is signed in to Play: plans show “Price unavailable” and no purchase can start; free features continue.

ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION. Access: the location permission Android requires before an app may read Wi-Fi names and nearby networks. Feature: the Wi-Fi name in Connectivity and scanning in the Pro Version Wi-Fi Analyzer. Data: local only; precise location is not stored or uploaded. Optionality: runtime permission, requested only when you open one of those features. If denied: the Wi-Fi name stays hidden and the analyzer cannot scan; the app offers Grant Permission or Open Settings, and other features keep working.

NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES. Access: nearby Wi-Fi devices. Feature: the Pro Version Analyzer list on Android 13 and later. Data: local only. Optionality: runtime permission, requested only for the analyzer. If denied: the nearby list is unavailable and other features keep working.

BLUETOOTH_CONNECT. Access: the Bluetooth on/off state. Feature: the Connectivity reading on Android 12 and later. Data: local only; the app does not scan for, pair with or connect to Bluetooth devices. Optionality: runtime permission, requested only when reading Bluetooth state. If denied: Bluetooth state shows “Not available on this device”.

RECORD_AUDIO. Access: microphone levels while the test is open. Feature: the microphone test. Data: the level stays in memory; audio is never recorded to a file, stored or sent. Optionality: runtime permission, requested after an explanation and only when you start the test. If denied: the test shows “Not tested” and offers a shortcut to Android Settings.

PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS (Usage Access). Access: app usage and package-size data through a special Android Settings access. Feature: app sizes, screen time and permission analysis in the Apps tab. Data: local only. Optionality: special access that you grant or revoke in Android Settings; the app does not read contacts, SMS, phone state, camera or private app files. If denied: usage insights show a banner with a Settings shortcut and the rest of the app works.

Advertising and your choices

The app is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may collect or receive your device’s advertising ID, basic device and app information, approximate location and diagnostics, as described in Google’s own privacy policy.

Where required by law, for example in the EEA and the UK, the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before requesting an ad. You can change or withdraw that choice later. When Google reports that a privacy-options form is required, a “Manage ad choices” button appears at the end of this policy and opens Google’s form.

On a free install, Download Text, Generate PDF and Download PDF each ask for one optional rewarded video. The action runs only after Google confirms the reward. If the video cannot load, is closed early or fails, nothing is exported and the app explains that you can try again. Remove Ads and Pro Version skip these videos.

If you buy Remove Ads or Pro Version, the app stops requesting ads and hides the ad-choices button because there are no ads to configure. Android Settings still controls your advertising ID for other apps.

You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

Remove Ads and Pro Version purchases

Remove Ads and Pro Version are separate one-time, lifetime, non-consumable purchases handled by Google Play. Remove Ads turns ads off. Pro Version turns ads off and unlocks Pro Version tools: the 2x2 and 4x2 widgets, extra accent colours, dashboard layout editing, the Animations Off setting, the Wi-Fi Analyzer and custom PDF/JSON report exports. There is no subscription and nothing renews.

The price shown for each plan is the localized price Google Play returns for your account and region. If Google Play returns no price, the plan says “Price unavailable” and cannot be bought. Google processes the payment; Device Matrix never receives or stores your card number or full billing address.

To decide which features are available, the app asks Google Play whether the Google account signed in on this phone owns each product. If you own Remove Ads and later buy the Pro Version upgrade, that combination counts as Pro Version. Aside from the protected local proof described above, the app sends no purchase information to us and has no purchase backend of its own.

Reinstalling the app or using another phone signed in to the same Google account can restore these purchases through Google Play. A different Google account does not inherit them. Refunds and payment disputes are handled by Google Play; if a purchase is refunded, ads can return and Pro Version features can lock again after the next ownership check.

If Google Play cannot be reached, a verified local proof for each product can keep a plan active for up to 30 days. After that, the app waits for Google Play to confirm the purchase again before keeping ads off or unlocking Pro Version features. A purchase is never granted by the JavaScript interface alone.

Home-screen widgets

Device Matrix can place home-screen widgets for RAM, storage, battery and a health summary. They read the same local values as the app and show them in your launcher. Nothing from a widget is uploaded.

The 2x2 and 4x2 widget sizes are Pro Version features. On a Free or Remove Ads install, Android draws those widgets with a locked message; the smaller sizes stay free. The native widget renderer checks the protected local proof, not a setting that JavaScript can simply switch on.

The storage unit and widget appearance choices are kept in the app’s private storage so widgets format sizes and colours consistently. Android and your launcher decide when a widget refreshes; the app requests an update about every 30 minutes. A widget may therefore show the values from its last update until the launcher refreshes it.

Widgets cannot be added from inside the app. You add them from your launcher’s widget picker.

Children

Device Matrix is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Keeping and deleting data

Because there is no backend, there is no server-side copy of your readings to delete. Local settings, saved test results, widget preferences and the Remove Ads or Pro Version purchase proofs remain in the app’s private storage until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. Reports you copied, shared or saved to Downloads are controlled by you and by the app or service you sent them to.

Deleting the app does not delete the purchase from your Google Play account. After reinstalling, sign in with the same account and use Restore purchase. Google Play keeps its own purchase records under Google’s terms and privacy policy.

Android protects app-private storage, but no software can promise perfect security. Keep your phone and its screen lock up to date.
