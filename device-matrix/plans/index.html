Ads on Free and optional Remove ads and Pro purchases help support the continued development, maintenance and improvement of Device Matrix.

Good to know

Purchases stay in Google Play.

Google Play shows the price

Google Play displays the local price and processes the payment. This website does not show a price or take payment.

No subscription or renewal

Remove ads and Pro are one-time purchases. There is no recurring charge and nothing to cancel.

Restore with the same account

Install the app on a new phone, sign in to the Google account used for the purchase and choose Restore purchase on the Pro screen.

If Google Play returns no price, the app shows “Price unavailable” and disables buying. All paid features are optional.

Questions

Plans FAQ.

Does the website show prices?

No. Google Play shows the localized price before purchase. If Google Play returns no price, the app displays “Price unavailable” and disables buying.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Remove ads and Pro are one-time purchases. There is no renewal or recurring charge.

What stays free?

Every standard information screen, every guided hardware test, text reports, the standard PDF and the 2×1 and 4×1 RAM, storage and battery widgets.

Can I restore a purchase on a new phone?

Yes, with the same Google account. Open the Pro screen and choose Restore purchase.

Does buying Pro change the readings?

No. Pro adds the Wi-Fi analyzer, larger widgets, custom exports, multiple theme colors, dashboard layout and Animations Off option. The standard readings and guided tests stay the same.
